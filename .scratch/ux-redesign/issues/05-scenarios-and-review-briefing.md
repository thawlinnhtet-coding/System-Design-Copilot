# 05 - Scenarios And Review Briefing

**What to build:** Redesign Scenario and Review surfaces around pressure-testing, evidence, uncertainty, and actionable change rather than gamified scoring.

**Blocked by:** 01 - Design Foundations And Brand; 02 - Workspace Stage Flow.

**Status:** complete

**Implementation edge:** 21 - Architecture Review Entry.

## Comments

- 2026-08-16: Scenario pressure-test and Review surfaces are integrated into the Workspace stages with evidence-oriented findings, uncertainty, prioritized actions, immutable history, retry states, and comparison framing that avoids causal score claims.

- [ ] Stress-test entry visibly switches into a focused Scenario workspace and explains the changed condition and impact without revealing a solution.
- [ ] Architecture Review Workspace entry captures the Review Brief before manual reconstruction begins.
- [ ] The User can explain or adapt one decision and return to the Workspace with the response attached.
- [ ] Completed Reviews present strengths, risks, evidence, uncertainty, and prioritized next actions as an evidence briefing.
- [ ] Findings jump to cited Requirements, Assumptions, Decisions, Components, Connections, or Scenario responses.
- [ ] Review comparison presents resolved, introduced, and still-uncertain evidence as a change briefing.
- [ ] The interface does not imply causal improvement from score movement alone.
- [ ] Loading, unavailable, retryable failure, and final failure states use the shared status and motion patterns.
- [ ] Review is a focused evidence briefing rather than a score dashboard or chat transcript.
- [ ] Review history and comparison remain inside the Workspace Review stage.
